## Screenshot visual rules

- real production UI only;
- no phone-in-phone mockups inside the app screenshot;
- captions outside the screenshot area or in store-frame composition;
- bright editorial brand system, not dark cosmic templates;
- one feature per frame;
- RU / EN / ES screenshots are independently typeset;
- do not bake misleading results or fake user data;
- no subscription claims until plans and prices are final.

## Feature graphic brief

- bright white or milk-white base;
- one expressive editorial illustration from the project asset system;
- product name plus one promise;
- no galaxy, zodiac wheel, crystals or fortune-teller props;
- no dense list of features;
- must remain readable at small Play Store preview size.

Working RU promise: `Личный гороскоп, который говорит по делу`.
