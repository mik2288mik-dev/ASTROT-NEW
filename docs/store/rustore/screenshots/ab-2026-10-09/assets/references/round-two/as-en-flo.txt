Why choose Flo Health for period and ovulation tracking?
Flo Health is more than a basic calendar — it's a period tracker and ovulation tracker designed to help you notice patterns over time.

- Over 70 trackable symptoms and moods to receive personalized menstrual cycle insights
- Support for irregular periods, pre-period symptoms, and cycle changes

Can Flo Health support my trying-to-conceive (TTC) journey?
Flo Health offers educational features to help you explore conception insights and better understand how to spot fertile signs.

- Fertile window and ovulation predictions based on your logged period data
- Log LH/ovulation test strip results and basal body temperature
- Discharge tracking to help you recognize fertile signs

How does Flo Health support me in pregnancy?
Once you conceive, switch to Pregnancy Mode for checklists and expert guidance.

- Pregnancy tracking with weekly updates on your baby's development
- Daily articles explaining fetal development at each stage

Can Flo Health help me with perimenopause symptoms?
Track changes during perimenopause with tools designed to help you better understand this life stage.

- Perimenopause Score to follow symptom changes
- Menopause Timeline to understand the perimenopause phases
- Tracker to log sleep, energy, mood, and well-being changes

Can I share with my partner?
Flo for Partners lets you invite your partner to support your health journey.

- Help them understand your menstrual cycle, get ovulation predictions, and celebrate pregnancy progress together.

Is Flo Health free?
The Flo Health app is free to download and includes core tools you need to track and understand your cycle.
