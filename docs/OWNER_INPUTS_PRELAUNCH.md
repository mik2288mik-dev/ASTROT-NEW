# Owner inputs required before production launch

Everything else can be prepared without guessing. These facts must come from the project owner before legal pages, store forms and production website go live.

## Identity

- Legal operator type: individual / sole proprietor / company.
- Full legal name.
- Country of establishment/residence.
- Legal/postal address if required for the selected jurisdiction.
- Public support name shown to users.

## Contacts

- Public support email.
- Privacy/data-rights email, if different.
- Optional press/partnership email.

## Domain and stores

- Final domain.
- Google Play URL.
- RuStore URL.
- App Store URL when available.

## Subscription

- Monthly plan and local price strategy.
- Annual plan and local price strategy.
- Trial: yes/no, duration and eligibility.
- Introductory offer: yes/no.
- Exact Premium feature list at launch.

## Data and providers

Confirm the actual production providers used at launch:

- hosting;
- database;
- AI provider(s);
- analytics;
- crash reporting;
- push notifications;
- email/support service;
- payments/subscription validation;
- CDN/storage.

## Policy decisions

- Minimum user age.
- Primary governing law/jurisdiction.
- Retention period for deleted-account backups/logs where technically necessary.
- Support response target.
- Account deletion processing target.
- Whether marketing email/push consent will exist in V1.

## Do not invent

No production policy may contain fictional company details, fake addresses, guessed processors, guessed retention periods, guessed subscription prices or unsupported security claims.
