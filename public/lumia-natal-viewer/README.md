# Lumia natal viewer assets

Put the final approved WebP images for the natal card viewer in this folder.

Required files:

- `cover.webp` - promo cover for the map screen.
- `first-impression.webp` - card 1, new people and first contact.
- `decisions.webp` - card 2, decision making.
- `communication.webp` - card 3, communication.
- `relationships.webp` - card 4, relationships.
- `blockers.webp` - card 5, blockers and pressure.
- `strengths.webp` - card 6, strongest practical zones.

Recommended export:

- WebP, 1440 x 1920 for cover.
- WebP, 1200 x 720 or larger for card heroes.
- Keep the center readable and avoid small text baked into the image.
