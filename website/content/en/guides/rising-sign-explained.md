The rising sign is the zodiac sign appearing on the eastern horizon at birth. It depends on date, time, and place, so it cannot be determined from a birthday alone.

## What it describes

In traditional interpretation, the ascendant is linked to first impressions, the way someone enters a new situation, and the instinctive style of responding before long reflection.

## How it differs from the Sun sign

The Sun sign is often connected with identity and direction. The rising sign is more about contact with the outside world. They can reinforce each other or look very different.

## Why it changes quickly

Because Earth rotates, the rising sign changes roughly every two hours. Even a 15–20 minute difference can affect the degree and house cusps, and near a sign boundary it may change the ascendant itself.

## Not a mask

The popular formula “Sun is the real you, rising sign is the mask” is too simple. Both are parts of one chart and can appear in different contexts.

When birth time is unknown, a responsible service should not invent an ascendant. It should either ask for better data or clearly limit the reading.
