Compatibility is often reduced to a number: 82%, 91%, “perfect match.” It fits neatly on a card, but real relationships are more complicated.

## What is compared

Synastry compares two natal charts. It looks at communication, emotional reactions, closeness, initiative, boundaries, and expectations. One contact may create strong attraction, another easy conversation, and another a recurring disagreement.

## Tension does not automatically mean failure

A challenging aspect can bring irritation, but it can also create movement, interest, and growth. A supportive aspect can make connection easier, yet it does not guarantee maturity or loyalty.

## More useful than a score

A useful reading answers concrete questions: Where do two people understand each other quickly? Where do they speak different emotional languages? What happens during conflict? What helps repair connection?

## What compatibility cannot see

A chart does not know the full history of a relationship, safety, abuse, addiction, or whether someone takes responsibility. Astrology should never be used to excuse harmful behaviour.

Compatibility works best as a map of differences, not a judge. Good relationships depend on respect, agreements, and real actions.
