A birth date gives the position of most planets, but the exact time affects a major part of a personal chart. It influences the rising sign, house system, and sometimes the Moon.

## Rising sign and houses

The rising sign changes roughly every two hours. Houses also shift with time and location. Two people born on the same date in the same city can have a very different house layout if one was born in the morning and the other in the evening.

## Why a default time is misleading

Some services silently use 12:00 when the time is unknown. That is convenient for software, but it creates false precision. The user sees houses and a rising sign as if they were calculated from real data.

A better approach is to request the time or offer a clearly labelled “unknown time” mode that removes conclusions depending on it.

## What can still be calculated

Most planetary sign positions and many aspects remain available. A general personal profile may still be useful, but houses and the exact ascendant should be excluded. If the Moon changed signs that day, the interpretation may also need a range rather than a fixed answer.

## Where to look for the time

Check birth certificates, medical records, family notes, or ask relatives. Even a time range is more honest than a random default, as long as the app explains the remaining uncertainty.
