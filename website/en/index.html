Nothing pulled from thin air.

Straight answers

What people usually ask before installing.

Is this a regular zodiac-sign horoscope?

No. Sign forecasts are the quick format, while the core product is based on your personal birth details.

Do I need my exact birth time?

It is strongly recommended for a full natal chart. You can still start without it, but some parts will be limited.

Will the app tell me something “must happen”?

No. Astrology here is a way to look at tendencies and reactions, not a timetable of inevitable events.

What will be free?

Part of the daily reading and basic features will be free. Full readings and expanded tools will be included in Premium.
